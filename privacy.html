Privacy Policy for Add-Venture

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Summary: Add-Venture is a 100% offline educational game for Grade 1 pupils. We do NOT collect, transmit, store on servers, or share any personal information, device data, or location data.

1. Introduction

Add-Venture ("we", "our", or "us") is an offline, game-based mobile learning application developed for Grade 1 pupils (ages 6–7) under the DepEd MATATAG Curriculum Framework. We are deeply committed to safeguarding the privacy of our learners, their parents, and educators. This Privacy Policy details our privacy standards and data handling practices.

2. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA & Families Policy)

Our application is designed specifically for children under the age of 13. We strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy:

  • We do not collect personal names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • We do not collect persistent device identifiers (such as IMEI, Android Advertising ID, or MAC addresses).
  • We do not require user account registration or social media login.
  • We do not contain third-party SDKs, analytics tracking, or behavioral profiling tools.

3. Information Collection & Usage

Add-Venture is an offline application. All data created during gameplay remains strictly on the user's local device:

  • Local Progress Data: Game stars earned, stages unlocked (Apple Orchard, Orange Grove, Harmony Tree), accuracy statistics, and in-game audio preferences (e.g. background music toggle) are saved exclusively on your local device storage using standard Android local storage (AsyncStorage).
  • No Server Transmission: None of this gameplay data is transmitted across the internet or uploaded to any remote server.

4. Advertisements & In-App Purchases

Add-Venture is completely free of commercial monetization:

  • Zero Advertisements: There are no banner ads, interstitial ads, video ads, or targeted advertisements of any kind.
  • Zero In-App Purchases: All learning modes, hints, and narrator features are 100% free and permanently unlocked through learner progress.

5. Third-Party Access

Because Add-Venture does not collect or transmit data, no user data is shared, sold, rented, or disclosed to third parties, partners, or advertising networks.

6. Diagnostic Report Export Feature

The application includes an optional feature in the Advanced Analytics screen allowing parents or teachers to view a text-based summary of learning competencies. When the user taps "Save / Share", the standard Android system share sheet is activated by the user. The app itself never transmits this report automatically.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted to this page with an updated effective date.
